# Risk register: what could stop the project or the claim, ranked

> Status: **register** (18 Oct 2026; R19 to R23 added 19 Oct 2026, R24 on 20 Oct 2026). One page of risks to the **project** (the schedule, the parts, the one person) and to the **claim** (what the design says it does), each with a likelihood and an impact on a three-point scale (L, M, H), a mitigation, a trigger and a state. The scores are **my judgement**, written for the owner to change; there is no data behind a likelihood. Date of the milestones: `STATUS.md` section 5 (M2 20 Oct to 3 Nov; v1.0 mid-December 2026).
